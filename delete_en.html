GO UP – Request data deletion

Option 1: in the Game (fastest)

Tap Settings > Delete my data and confirm. Your ranking records, nickname reservation, cloud backup, recovery code, cheers you sent or received and your anonymous account are deleted. Data stored on your device is cleared too.

Option 2: by email

If you cannot open the Game (for example, you uninstalled it), email contact@localfeedback.app with the subject "GO UP data deletion". So we can find your data, please include your nickname and, if you have it, your recovery code. Do not send passwords or payment details; we never ask for them. We will reply after deleting the data, normally within 30 days.

What remains

Reports you submitted or received are deleted automatically 90 days after they are created. Your advertising ID is held by Google (AdMob), not by us, so reset or delete it in your device settings. Purchase records are kept by Google Play under its own policies.
