GO UP Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-27. This policy explains what information the mobile game "GO UP" (the "Game"), provided by LocalFeedback ("we"), processes and why. Game version 1.0.0 does not use any analytics service.

1. What we collect and why

InformationPurpose
Nickname (entered by you, 2–12 characters)Profile and ranking display. Visible to other players.
Country (a 2-letter code taken from your device language/region setting; this is not location data)Flag display and country rankings. Shown publicly in the rankings.
Ranking profile: distance, equipped flag, character and flagpoleRanking display. Visible to other players.
Game records: distance, coins, stars, owned and equipped items, settings, discoveries, play timeSaving progress, cloud backup and restore
Anonymous account ID: created automatically by Firebase Authentication. There is no sign-up, email or password.Linking ranking records and backups to you, preventing abuse
Recovery code (16 characters) and the sign-in token stored with itRestoring your backup on a new device. Your recovery code works like a password. Never share it with anyone. Anyone who knows the code can restore your data.
Cheers and reports: a cheer is one of 4 fixed phrases; a report contains the reported ID, a short reason, the reporter ID and the time.Social features and moderation (for example, handling inappropriate nicknames). Report records are kept for 90 days for moderation and then deleted.
Advertising ID and device and ad-interaction information (collected by Google AdMob)Serving ads (only ads you choose to watch) and measuring their performance. Personalized ads are shown by default. In regions where consent is required, such as the European Economic Area and the UK, a Google consent form appears the first time you launch the Game and your choice is followed. You can switch to Limited ads (non-personalized) at any time in Settings > Privacy choices, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
Purchase information (Google Play billing): purchase token and product IDDelivering and restoring purchased items, preventing duplicate delivery. We do not receive card or payment details; Google Play handles them.

We do not ask for your real name, email, phone number, precise location, photos or contacts. Photo mode only saves a picture to your device when you press the shutter; it does not read your gallery.

2. Third parties that process information

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore): stores the information above for rankings, cheers, reports and cloud backup.
  • Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform: rewarded ads and the ad consent form.
  • Google Play: purchase processing.

Each provider processes information under its own privacy policy. Data is sent over HTTPS. We do not sell personal information and do not use our own analytics or crash-reporting service.

3. How long we keep data

Server data (ranking records, nickname reservation, cloud backup, recovery code, cheers) is kept until you delete it or ask us to delete it. Report records are deleted 90 days after they are created. Data stored on your device is removed when you delete the Game.

4. How to delete your data

In the Game, tap Settings > Delete my data. Your ranking records, nickname reservation, cloud backup, recovery code, cheers you sent or received and your anonymous account are deleted, and data on your device is cleared. You can also send a request to contact@localfeedback.app (Data deletion). Your advertising ID is held by Google, so manage it in your device settings.

5. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, please contact us and we will delete it.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date.
