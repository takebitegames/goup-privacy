GO UP – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-24. This policy explains what information the mobile game "GO UP" (the "Game") by TakeBite Games ("we") handles and why.

1. Information we collect

ItemPurpose
Nickname you enterShown on your profile and rankings
Country (from device/locale settings)Country flag and country rankings
Game records (distance, coins, stars, owned items, settings)Progress, rankings, cloud backup and restore
Advertising ID (via Google AdMob)Showing ads and measuring ads. You can limit or reset it in your device settings and in the in-game "Privacy choices".
Purchase history (via Google Play)Delivering and restoring purchased items. We do not receive your card or payment details; Google Play handles payment.

We do not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, precise location, or contacts.

2. Third-party services

We use Google Play (purchases, sign-in for backup), Firebase (data storage for rankings and backup, basic analytics) and Google AdMob (ads). These providers process data under their own privacy policies.

3. Retention

Data is kept while you play and until you delete it. When you request deletion, we delete the data stored on our servers and on your device.

4. How to delete your data

In the Game, open Settings > Delete my data. You can also email us at the address below and we will delete it on request.

5. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date.

7. Contact
