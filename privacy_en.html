GO UP – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-26. This policy explains what information the mobile game "GO UP" (the "Game") by TakeBite Games ("we") handles and why. Version 1.0.0 of the Game does not use any analytics service.

1. Information we collect

ItemPurpose
Nickname you enter (2–12 characters)Your profile and the rankings. Other players can see it.
Country (a 2-letter code from your device language/region setting, not your location)Country flag and country rankings. Shown publicly in rankings.
Ranking profile: distance, flag, character and pole you equippedRankings. Shown to other players.
Game records: distance, coins, stars, owned and equipped items, settings, discoveries, play timeSaving progress, cloud backup and restore
Anonymous account ID, created automatically by Firebase Authentication. There is no sign-up and no email or password.Linking your ranking entry and backup to you, and preventing abuse
Recovery code (16 characters) and the sign-in token stored with itRestoring your backup on a new device. Treat the code like a password and never share it. Anyone who has the code can restore your data.
Cheers and reports: a cheer is one of 4 preset messages; a report contains the reported player's ID, a short reason, your ID and the timeSocial features and moderation. Reports are kept for operations (for example, handling inappropriate nicknames) for 90 days and then deleted.
Advertising ID and device/ad interaction data (collected by Google AdMob)Showing ads (only ads you choose to watch) and measuring them. You can choose limited, non-personalized ads in the consent dialog and later in Settings > Privacy choices, and you can reset or delete the advertising ID in your device settings.
Purchase information (via Google Play Billing): a purchase token and product IDDelivering and restoring purchased items and preventing double delivery. We never receive your card or payment details; Google Play handles payment.

We do not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, precise location, photos, or contacts. The photo mode only saves a picture to your device when you press the shutter; we do not read your gallery.

2. Who processes the data

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore): stores the data above for rankings, cheers, reports and cloud backup.
  • Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform: rewarded ads and the ad-consent dialog.
  • Google Play: purchases.

These providers process data under their own privacy policies. Data is sent over HTTPS. We do not sell personal information and we do not use any analytics or crash-reporting service of our own.

3. Retention

Server data (ranking entry, nickname reservation, cloud backup, recovery code, cheers) is kept until you delete it or ask us to delete it. Reports are deleted 90 days after they are created. Data on your device is removed when you uninstall the Game.

4. How to delete your data

In the Game, open Settings > Delete my data. This deletes your ranking entry, nickname reservation, cloud backup, recovery code, cheers you sent or received, and your anonymous account, and clears the data on your device. You can also email dev@playgoup.com – see Data deletion. The advertising ID is held by Google; manage it in your device settings.

5. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date.
